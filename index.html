2026 WSDip Key Timeline & Intake Process

3 NOV - 12 DEC 2025 & 5 JAN - 27 FEB 2026
Step 1: Online Registration & Application
Applicants (ITE fresh graduates or in-service employees) apply on the ITE iStudent Portal. They can select up to 5 WSDip course preferences. Discretionary admissions must secure employer sponsorship beforehand.
NOV 2025 - FEB 2026
Step 2: Profile Setup & Employer Matching
Trainees set up profiles on the WSDip Connect Portal. Partner employers view applications, screen resumes, and conduct interviews to hire successful candidates as full-time staff.
MID FEB - MAR 2026
Step 3: Offer Acceptance & LOC Signing
Successful applicants accept the job offer and WSDip sponsorship. Partner companies submit the Letter of Commitment (LOC) via the WSDC company representative account, securing the trainee's place.
EARLY APRIL 2026
Step 4: Academic Commencement
Classes begin. Trainees start their 30-month work-study cycle. Typically, this is 1 to 1.5 days at ITE for theory/practical studies, and remaining days at the company conducting structured On-the-Job Training.

Financial Incentives (Singapore Citizens)

SkillsFuture Sign-on Incentive ($5,000)

Eligible for Singapore Citizen fresh graduates (within 36 months of graduation or ORD). Disbursed in two stages: $2,500 at 6 months after starting the course, and $2,500 upon graduation. *(Not eligible for discretionary admission path).*

ITE Progression Award (IPA) ($10,000)

For Singapore Citizen graduates aged 30 and below who enroll in WSDip. Trainees receive a $10,000 top-up directly to their CPF Ordinary Account (OA) upon graduation to boost home ownership/wealth building.

Company Sponsorship & CPF

Trainees are hired as full-time staff receiving standard employee benefits (annual/medical leave, CPF contributions). The employer fully sponsors the program course fees throughout the 2.5-year duration.
